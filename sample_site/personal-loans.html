Personal loans

Fixed-rate loans with the same monthly payment for the whole term.

Northwind Personal Loan

  • Borrow from $2,000 to $40,000
  • Fixed APR from 7.49% to 18.99%, based on your credit profile
  • Terms from 12 to 60 months
  • Origination fee: 0% to 4% of the loan amount
  • Late payment fee: $25
  • No prepayment penalty

Representative example: a $10,000 loan over 36 months at 9.99% APR has a monthly payment of $322.62. The total amount repaid is $11,614.32.

Eligibility: US residents aged 18 or over with a credit score of 640 or higher.

Northwind Home Improvement Loan

  • Borrow from $10,000 to $75,000
  • Fixed APR from 6.99%
  • Terms up to 84 months
  • No origination fee

Eligibility: homeowners with a credit score of 680 or higher. Funds must be used for home repairs or improvements.
