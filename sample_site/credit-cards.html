Northwind credit cards

Compare our three credit cards. All rates are variable and based on the Prime Rate.

Northwind Everyday Card

A simple card for everyday spending with no annual fee.

  • Purchase APR: 19.9% variable
  • Annual fee: $0
  • Balance transfer fee: 3% of each transfer (minimum $5)
  • Foreign transaction fee: 2.5% of each transaction
  • Earn 1% cashback on groceries and fuel

Eligibility: you must be at least 18 years old with an annual income of at least $20,000.

Northwind Travel Rewards Card

For frequent travellers who want to earn points on trips and dining.

  • Purchase APR: 22.4% variable
  • Annual fee: $95
  • No foreign transaction fees
  • Earn 2x points on travel and dining, 1x on everything else
  • Welcome bonus: 40,000 points after you spend $3,000 in the first 3 months

Eligibility: annual income of at least $40,000 and a good credit history.

Northwind Student Card

A first credit card for students enrolled in college or university.

  • Purchase APR: 17.9% variable
  • Annual fee: $0
  • Credit limit up to $1,500
  • Late payment fee: up to $25

Eligibility: aged 18 or over and currently enrolled as a student.
