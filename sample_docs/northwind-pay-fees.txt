NORTHWIND PAY DIGITAL WALLET

Sample document: Northwind Community Bank is fictional and all figures are invented for testing. Not financial advice.

Northwind Pay is a digital wallet for sending money to friends and paying in stores with your phone.

FEES

- Standard transfers to a bank account (1-3 business days): free
- Instant transfers to a debit card: 1.5% of the amount (minimum $0.25, maximum $15)
- International transfers: $4.99 per transfer plus a 1% currency conversion fee
- Card payments in stores: free

LIMITS

- Daily sending limit: $2,500
- Monthly sending limit: $10,000
- Verified accounts can raise the daily limit to $5,000

ELIGIBILITY

You must be 18 or older, live in the United States and link a Northwind debit card or checking account.
