# Northwind Certificates of Deposit: Rate Sheet

> Sample document: Northwind Community Bank is fictional and all figures are invented for testing.
> Not financial advice.

Certificates of deposit (CDs) pay a fixed rate for the whole term. Interest compounds daily and is
credited monthly.

## 12-Month CD

- 4.35% APY, fixed for 12 months
- Minimum deposit: $1,000
- Early withdrawal penalty: 90 days of interest
- Renews automatically at maturity unless you tell us otherwise within a 10-day grace period

## 60-Month CD

- 3.75% APY, fixed for 60 months
- Minimum deposit: $1,000
- Early withdrawal penalty: 365 days of interest

## Eligibility

CDs are open to individuals aged 18 or over with a Northwind checking or savings account.
